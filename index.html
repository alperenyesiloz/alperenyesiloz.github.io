shorts-factory

shorts-factory is a private, personal tool used by its owner to publish short educational videos about interesting facts to the owner's own YouTube channel.

It is not a public service. It is not offered to other users, and no one else can sign in to it.

What it does with the YouTube API:

  • Uploads videos created by the owner to the owner's own channel.
  • Reads analytics of the owner's own channel to improve future videos.
  • Reads comments on the owner's own videos to detect reported factual errors.
